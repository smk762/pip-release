# Social publishing tools with human approval

Research date: 25 October 2026. Prices and network lists below are from pages fetched that day unless a source states its own date. Where a page’s feature-matrix checkmarks did not survive extraction, that is called out instead of inferred.

Job being compared: watch a PyPI user’s package releases, draft an announcement, let a human edit or approve it, then publish, with more networks addable later. The four networks that matter most are X (Twitter), Discord, Telegram, and Patreon.

Label used throughout:

- **Full match for the approval half:** an external item can become post text without a person writing it, the post is held so it will not go live, and a person can edit it and then approve publishing to more than one network.
- **Partial — approval queue only:** a person writes or pastes the post, and a reviewer must approve it. Ingest, if any, does not wait.
- **Partial — scheduler only:** the product schedules or auto-publishes text a person (or a Zap) already supplied. No documented hold.

## Which products have a real approval queue?

### Takeaway

SocialBee is the only named product whose own help docs describe imported RSS and Zapier items sitting as drafts that will not publish until someone marks them approved. Publer, Mixpost Pro, Buffer Team, Hootsuite Advanced, Loomly, Vista Social, Later, Sendible, and Sprinklr all document a real review step for posts, but most of those queues are for posts a person created. Hypefury documents scheduling and automation, not an approval queue. Typefully’s API can leave a draft “planned,” meaning dated but not auto-published, which is a hold, but it is not described as a multi-person approval queue. Postiz documents agents and a calendar, and its own comparison copy says a person approves in the calendar, but its plans and webhook docs do not describe a status that blocks publishing.

### Cited Findings

- **SocialBee — full match for the hold.** Unapproved posts are drafts and “will not be shared until you set them to Approved.” The Content Approval tab can be filtered by source, and the listed sources include RSS feeds, Zapier, CSV, media import, link import, Quuu, and the web app. The same help article says the feature is for “importing content through various sources (RSS Feeds, Quuu, Bulk imports, or Zapier).” — [SocialBee content approval](https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/29979144914711-Content-Approval-for-Your-Posts)
- SocialBee’s own product FAQ says post approvals are **not** on Bootstrap ($29/month) and **are** on Accelerate ($49/month). Internal notes are Pro ($99/month) and above. This is a draft/approved toggle and a queue, not a documented multi-stage chain. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/)
- **Publer — real approval queue on Business, separate from RSS automation.** Business adds “advanced team collaboration with roles and permission levels and post approval workflows.” Professional includes unlimited RSS automations that auto-schedule or auto-post, and does not list approval workflows. Zapier’s Publer actions include “Create Post (publish now, schedule, or save as draft)” and triggers “Post Pending Approval” and “Post Approved.” — [Publer plan comparison](https://publer.com/help/en/article/professional-vs-business-vs-enterprise-plans-1sge3f7/); [Publer Business contents](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-is-included-in-publer-business-1c57hsq/); [Zapier Publer + RSS](https://zapier.com/apps/publer-k20/integrations/rss/255714441/schedule-publer-posts-from-new-rss-feed-items)
- **Mixpost Pro — documented approval flow.** Pro includes “Approval flow.” The product describes routing drafts through review “before anything goes live.” The API `POST /:workspace/posts/approve/:post` approves a post that is in `needs_approval` and moves it to `scheduled`. Lite’s feature list does not include approval. — [Mixpost pricing](https://mixpost.app/pricing); [Mixpost automation](https://mixpost.app/social-media-automation); [Approve a post](https://docs.mixpost.app/api/reference/approve-post)
- **Buffer — approval on Team only.** The pricing page lists “Content approval workflows” under Team and not under Free or Essentials. Drafts as a storage feature exist on the lower plans; that is not the same as the approval workflow. — [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing)
- **Hootsuite — approval on Advanced only.** Advanced is described as “Review and approve content with your team,” including approval workflows, internal comments, and suspending scheduled posts. Standard and Professional descriptions on the same page do not include that. — [Hootsuite pricing](https://www.hootsuite.com/pricing)
- **Loomly — approval on the entry paid plan.** The pricing page lists “Approval workflows and roles” under Starter key features, and “Custom roles & workflows” under Beyond. — [Loomly pricing](https://www.loomly.com/pricing)
- **Vista Social — single-stage and multi-stage rows exist.** The live pricing comparison lists “Single-stage approval workflows” and “Multi-stage approval workflows” as separate collaboration features, plus draft posts and post comments. Checkmarks did not survive page extraction, so which plan includes multi-stage is not cleanly readable from that table. Vista’s own Later comparison says one stage is on Professional and multi-stage is on Advanced, but that article’s prices do not match the live pricing page (see pricing section). — [Vista Social pricing](https://vistasocial.com/pricing); [Vista Social vs Later](https://vistasocial.com/insights/vista-social-vs-later/)
- **Later — approval on Growth and Scale, with a hole.** Official help: content approval is Growth and Scale. A reviewer can Approve or Request changes. The same article says: “Scheduled posts will publish at their scheduled time regardless of approval status.” A draft, or a user whose “Publish Without Approval” permission is off, is what actually stops publishing. External reviewers can comment through a link; the pricing table calls external participation an “External Comment Workflow,” and the help article’s approval actions are for users with the Reviewer role. — [Later help: approvals](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/40954654500375-Team-Collaboration-and-Content-Approvals); [Later pricing](https://later.com/pricing/)
- **Sendible — assignment and custom approval from Plus up.** The pricing page puts “Assignment & approvals” on Plus, and the feature list includes “Assign posts for approval” and “Custom approval workflows” that can “restrict users to requiring approval on any post they schedule.” That wording is about posts users schedule, not about generated RSS items. — [Sendible pricing](https://www.sendible.com/pricing)
- **Sprinklr — real multi-step approval.** Help docs: a post “will be published on the social media channel only after it is approved.” Options are not required, required by account owner, or follow an approval path (tiered and parallel). Outbound rules can attach an approval path from conditions such as channel, account, and message type. The help article’s stamp read “Updated 3 days ago” when fetched on 25 October 2026. — [Sprinklr: set approval while publishing](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/approval-workflows/how-to-set-the-approval-workflows-while-publishing/6454897b0d27fc559bbeb45e); [Sprinklr publishing](https://www.sprinklr.com/products/social-media-management/social-media-publishing-platform/)
- **Typefully — inert planned drafts, plus comments.** The public API can create a draft that is saved, planned, scheduled, or published immediately. “Planned” means `plan_at` is “dated but inert — it never auto-publishes until confirmed.” A past planned date “is not overdue and not a failure.” WRITE permission can plan; PUBLISH permission is required to schedule or publish now. The API also supports comments on drafts. Webhooks are outbound (draft created, published, scheduled, status changed, tags changed, deleted). The pricing page lists team permissions and Slack notifications under collaboration. It does not describe a named approval queue. — [Typefully API docs](https://typefully.com/docs/api); [Typefully API help](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718287-typefully-api); [Typefully pricing](https://typefully.com/pricing)
- **Hypefury — scheduler, not an approval queue.** The features and pricing page documents unlimited scheduling, cross-posting, autoplugs, auto-DMs, and engagement watching. No approval, draft-hold, or review role was on that page. — [Hypefury features and pricing](https://hypefury.com/features-pricing/)
- **Postiz — calendar and agents, not a documented blocking queue.** Cloud plans list team members from Team upward, RSS auto-posting from Team upward, and the public API on every paid plan. Docs define RSS auto-posting as turning feeds into scheduled posts. Webhooks “fire when a post publishes.” Postiz’s own Mixpost comparison says agents “plan, draft and schedule posts … while you approve everything in a visual calendar.” That sentence is not backed by an approval-status doc in the plans or webhooks pages. — [Postiz plans](https://docs.postiz.com/cloud/plans); [Postiz webhooks](https://docs.postiz.com/general/automations/webhooks); [Postiz vs Mixpost](https://postiz.com/compare/postiz/mixpost)
- **OmniSocials — approval events exist on the API.** Webhook event names include `post.scheduled`, `post.published`, `post.failed`, `post.approved`, and `post.rejected`. The same page documents create, schedule, update, and publish. A human approval UI was not described on the page fetched. — [OmniSocials API](https://omnisocials.com/api)
- **Breakreach — API can save a draft.** `POST /v1/posts` can “Publish now, schedule, take the next free slot or save a draft.” `PATCH /v1/posts/:id` can edit a draft, scheduled, or failed post. The page says the network table was “Checked on October 12, 2026, against Breakreach's publishing code.” It does not describe a reviewer role. — [Breakreach posting API](https://www.breakreach.com/developers/posting-api)

### Inferences

- For this job, “a draft is generated and waits” is documented end to end only for SocialBee among the named social suites. The others either review human-written posts, or they auto-schedule an imported item.
- Later’s approval feature does not by itself meet the job. A scheduled post can still go out unapproved.
- Typefully’s planned state is a real hold for API-created text, useful if the human confirms inside Typefully, and it is narrower than a team approval product.
- Postiz’s “approve in the calendar” line should not be treated as a queue that stops an RSS item.

### Gaps

- Loomly’s pricing page confirms approval workflows. This pass did not open a Loomly doc showing whether an RSS or webhook item can be created already inside that workflow.
- Vista Social’s live matrix lists both approval types, but the checkmarks were lost in extraction, and Vista’s own narrative article disagrees with the live prices, so the plan that currently includes multi-stage approval is not settled.
- Sendible documents approval and a separate RSS auto-poster. This pass did not find a Sendible doc saying an RSS item is created as an unapproved draft.
- Sprinklr’s approval docs assume a person is in the publisher. This pass did not find an inbound “create post pending approval” API or RSS doc.
- Whether Buffer’s or Hootsuite’s APIs can create a post already in the approval state was not in the pages fetched. Their Zapier templates schedule or queue.
- Planable, SocialPilot, Agorapulse, Kontentino, and PostPlanify appear in a 2026 roundup with claimed approval prices. Those claims were not checked on each vendor’s site. — [PostPlanify roundup](https://postplanify.com/blog/best-social-media-tools-with-approval-workflows)

## Can a draft start from RSS, email, Zapier, Make, n8n, or a webhook?

### Takeaway

Yes for RSS and Zapier, with SocialBee the clearest case that the imported text can stay a draft. Publer, Buffer, Hootsuite, Sendible, Mixpost, and Postiz can all start a post from an RSS item or from Zapier, Make, n8n, or an API, but their native RSS features are described as auto-post or auto-schedule. No product page reviewed documents a native “email this to create a draft” inbox. Inbound webhooks that create a draft were not documented; the practical ingest path is the product’s API or a Zapier/Make/n8n action. PyPI already publishes a per-project release RSS feed, so any of the RSS ingests could watch a package without a PyPI-specific integration.

### Cited Findings

- **SocialBee RSS can be forced to drafts.** Setup is Content → RSS → Add RSS. “If you want the posts to be automatically shared, keep the Approved toggle as it is. If not, switch it to Drafts.” After the feed is added, SocialBee looks for new posts every hour. An article with no featured image and no link preview is added as not approved even if auto-approve is on. — [SocialBee RSS](https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/29979191413783-How-Do-I-Add-RSS-Feeds-in-SocialBee)
- **SocialBee Zapier can import as draft.** The only Zapier action is Create Post. The action fields include “Import as Approved or Draft.” The trigger can be “any app in Zapier.” Make and Pabbly are also listed as workflow integrations. — [SocialBee Zapier](https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/29979077611287-Integrations-How-Can-I-Use-the-Zapier-Integration); [SocialBee integrations](https://socialbee.com/integrations/); [Zapier RSS → SocialBee](https://zapier.com/apps/rss/integrations/socialbee/289196/create-socialbee-posts-from-new-items-in-feed)
- **Publer RSS does not wait.** RSS automation “will Auto-post, Auto-schedule, or take No Action.” Only articles published after the feed is connected are automated. “No Action” keeps items in the library for a person to share. That is a manual pick list, not the approval queue. — [Publer RSS](https://publer.com/help/en/article/rss-feeds-in-publer-tmy7cj/)
- **Publer Zapier can save a draft** from a new RSS item via Create Post, and a separate trigger fires when a post is pending approval. Those are two Zapier capabilities, not a documented native “RSS item enters the approval queue” setting. — [Zapier Publer + RSS](https://zapier.com/apps/publer-k20/integrations/rss/255714441/schedule-publer-posts-from-new-rss-feed-items)
- **Buffer has no native RSS auto-post.** Buffer’s help says to use Zapier to send RSS items to the queue, and points at a Zapier template. The Zapier action is “Add to Queue” or send immediately. A third-party walkthrough of that template says it does not add a human approval step. — [Buffer + Zapier](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-zapier-with-buffer-EapRng2lI7); [Zapier RSS → Buffer](https://zapier.com/apps/buffer/integrations/rss/1565622/add-new-items-from-multiple-rss-feeds-to-buffer-queues)
- **Hootsuite Zapier schedules.** The template is “Schedule or publish new RSS items on Hootsuite.” The action is Schedule Message, with text, social profile, and “Post immediately?”. It does not describe creating an item in the Advanced approval workflow. — [Zapier RSS → Hootsuite](https://zapier.com/apps/hootsuite/integrations/rss/18484/schedule-or-publish-new-rss-items-on-hootsuite)
- **Sendible RSS auto-posts.** The pricing feature list includes “RSS/Blog feeds” (“source content by setting up RSS feeds”) and “RSS Auto-poster” (“Auto-post new articles from RSS feeds as soon as they’re published”). Zapier is also listed. The auto-poster wording is immediate publishing. — [Sendible pricing](https://www.sendible.com/pricing)
- **Postiz RSS becomes scheduled posts, on Team and above.** “RSS auto-posting turns feeds into scheduled posts.” It is off on Standard ($29/month) and on for Team ($39), Pro ($49), and Ultimate ($99). Webhooks on Postiz are outbound publish notifications, available from Standard (2 webhooks) up. The docs say to use them to trigger Zapier or n8n after publish, which is the reverse direction. Postiz’s comparison page says it integrates with n8n and Make and offers a public API. — [Postiz plans](https://docs.postiz.com/cloud/plans); [Postiz webhooks](https://docs.postiz.com/general/automations/webhooks); [Postiz vs Mixpost](https://postiz.com/compare/postiz/mixpost)
- **Mixpost: API, n8n, and webhooks; RSS described as auto-publish.** The automation page says you can “Trigger posts from n8n, the REST API and webhooks, or the Mixpost MCP server.” The features page separately describes webhooks as “real-time notifications when posts are scheduled, when post published, or when users take actions,” and under content sources says “Auto-publish blog posts, product updates, or RSS feeds.” Those two webhook descriptions point in opposite directions. — [Mixpost automation](https://mixpost.app/social-media-automation); [Mixpost features](https://mixpost.app/features)
- **Typefully can be filled by API, not by a documented RSS importer.** Create-draft is `POST /v2/social-sets/{social_set_id}/drafts`. Zapier is named on the pricing page. Webhooks are outbound. — [Typefully API](https://typefully.com/docs/api); [Typefully pricing](https://typefully.com/pricing)
- **PyPI already emits RSS.** `https://pypi.org/rss/project/<package>/releases.xml` lists versions of one project. A 15 October 2026 article on postrss.com describes pointing an auto-poster at that URL. The article’s flow publishes automatically. It does not describe a human hold. — [Postrss: PyPI release RSS](https://postrss.com/pypi-release-rss-feed-auto-post/)
- **Email.** None of the vendor pages fetched document a native inbox that turns an email into a social draft. SocialBee’s Zapier doc does say the trigger can be any Zapier app, which would include email if the user builds that Zap. — [SocialBee Zapier](https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/29979077611287-Integrations-How-Can-I-Use-the-Zapier-Integration)

### Inferences

- A PyPI release feed can be the trigger for SocialBee without any PyPI-specific feature: set the feed’s approval toggle to Drafts, then edit and approve. Polling is hourly on SocialBee’s side.
- The same feed could call Publer or Typefully through Zapier or a small script and land as a draft or a planned post. That is a built workflow, not a native RSS-to-approval setting.
- Postiz and Sendible native RSS, and Buffer/Hootsuite Zapier templates, fight the approval requirement because they schedule or publish.
- Mixpost can likely sit in the middle because posts can be in `needs_approval` and the API can approve them, but the create-post payload that sets that status was not fetched, and the RSS sentence says auto-publish.

### Gaps

- No vendor page reviewed documents native email-to-draft.
- No vendor page reviewed documents an inbound webhook whose body becomes an unapproved draft. “Webhooks” on Postiz, Typefully, Buffer-adjacent tools, and Mixpost’s features page are outbound notifications. Mixpost’s automation page also says webhooks can trigger posts; the payload and whether the result waits for approval were not documented in the pages fetched.
- Later: no RSS or Zapier ingest was found on the pricing or approval help pages.
- Loomly, Vista Social, Hypefury, and Sprinklr: RSS or inbound automation was not confirmed on an official page in this pass. A third-party Sendible-vs-Vista table says Vista has RSS auto-post; that was not checked against Vista’s docs. — [Postozo comparison](https://postozo.com/compare/sendible-vs-vista-social/)
- SocialBee’s hour poll and Zapier’s 15-minute free-plan poll are the only cadences found. Neither is a push from PyPI.

### Inferences

- No reviewed product covers all four targets. The pair X + Discord + Telegram shows up together on Postiz, OmniSocials, Breakreach, and PostEverywhere. Adding a human hold to that pair is the missing piece in their docs, except OmniSocials’ `post.approved` / `post.rejected` events and Breakreach’s save-as-draft.
- Publer is the named suite that has both X and Telegram. It does not list Discord or Patreon.
- SocialBee, Buffer, and Mixpost cover X and not the chat networks.
- Patreon is not a realistic “add the network in settings” target. Even Patreon’s own API, on the evidence fetched, is not a post publisher. A browser automation product (Cotlet) is the only page that claimed it can publish to Patreon, and that is not a social scheduler.

### Gaps

- Hootsuite, Loomly, Sprinklr, and Sendible’s complete network lists were not captured from an official catalog page. Do not treat “and more” or silence as support for Discord, Telegram, or Patreon.
- Vista’s publishing page and FAQ overlap but are not identical (TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, Snapchat, and Tumblr appear on the publishing page and not in the FAQ sentence). Bluesky is claimed in Vista’s Later article and is not in the FAQ sentence. Discord, Telegram, and Patreon are absent from both.
- Postiz, OmniSocials, and Breakreach describe Telegram and Discord as bot or webhook destinations (a channel or group), not as user-account timelines. That still counts as publishing to those networks.

## What does the approval-and-automation tier cost, and is there a self-hosted option?

### Takeaway

The tier that includes a documented hold is often a higher plan: SocialBee Accelerate at $49/month, Publer Business from $10 per social account per month, Buffer Team at $10 per channel per month on annual billing, Hootsuite Advanced at $399 per user per month on annual billing, Loomly Starter at $65/month or $49/month yearly, Vista Social Professional at $99/month plus $29/month per X profile, Later Growth at $37.50/month on yearly billing, and Mixpost Pro at $299 once. Postiz Team at $39/month is the tier with RSS auto-posting, team members, Discord, and Telegram, but not a documented approval queue. Self-hosted options with first-party docs are Mixpost (Lite open source; approval only on paid Pro) and Postiz (self-host documented; cloud plans priced separately).

### Cited Findings

- **SocialBee.** Bootstrap $29/month, no post approvals. Accelerate $49/month, up to 10 profiles, post approvals, CSV, bulk edit. Pro $99/month, up to 25 profiles, 5 workspaces, 3 users, internal notes. Pro50 $179, Pro100 $329, Pro150 $449 per month. Annual billing saves 16%. Zapier, Make, and Pabbly are listed as integrations. Not described as self-hosted. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/); [SocialBee integrations](https://socialbee.com/integrations/)
- **Publer.** Help center: Professional “starts at $5/month for 1 social account” and includes X plus unlimited RSS auto-post or auto-schedule, not approval workflows. Business “starts at $10/month for 1 social account” and adds approval workflows and API access for customers in good standing. The plans page prices per social account, with every 10th account free, and additional members for collaboration. The plans page fetch showed “/ month billed yearly” without a rendered dollar amount, so the $5 and $10 figures are from the help article, not from the plans grid. ## Does any product mention PyPI, GitHub releases, or changelog announcements?

### Takeaway

None of Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, SocialBee, Publer, Typefully, Hypefury, Loomly, Sprinklr, Sendible, Vista Social, Mixpost, or Postiz mention PyPI, GitHub releases, or changelog announcements on the pages reviewed. A PyPI release RSS feed exists and is generic enough for any RSS importer. One niche auto-poster article (15 October 2026) is specifically about announcing PyPI releases and does not describe approval. A GitHub Action posts release notes immediately and does not wait for a person.

### Cited Findings

- PyPI project release feed shape: `https://pypi.org/rss/project/your-package/releases.xml`. Also `https://pypi.org/rss/updates.xml` for the whole index and `https://pypi.org/rss/packages.xml` for newly registered projects. The 15 October 2026 postrss.com article uses the project feed as an auto-post source and tells the reader not to paste the full release notes. — [Postrss: PyPI release RSS](https://postrss.com/pypi-release-rss-feed-auto-post/)
- Upload-Post documents a GitHub Action on `release: published` that posts `release.name`, `tag_name`, and `html_url` to X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Threads, “and more.” The sample workflow has no approval step. — [Upload-Post: GitHub Actions](https://www.upload-post.com/how-to/post-to-social-media-from-github-actions/)
- GitHub Marketplace “Crosspost Action” reads a sitemap or RSS/Atom feed and posts to “Twitter/X, Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn, etc.” It has a dry-run flag. Dry-run is a preview, not an approval queue inside a social product. — [Crosspost Action](https://github.com/marketplace/actions/crosspost-action)
- Mixpost publishes its own product changelog (for example Pro 3.0.0 on 31 October 2024 added the approval flow). That is Mixpost’s release notes, not a feature that announces a customer’s changelog. — [Mixpost Pro releases](https://mixpost.app/releases/pro)
- Searches and the vendor pages above for the named social suites did not turn up the words PyPI, GitHub releases, or changelog-announcement features.

### Inferences

- The job’s trigger is already solved at the source by PyPI’s RSS, and GitHub releases can be a webhook or an Action. The unsolved half inside existing products is the hold, plus Discord, Telegram, and Patreon as destinations.
- SocialBee plus the PyPI project RSS feed is the closest off-the-shelf version of “draft, human edits, publish to several networks,” and those networks do not include Discord, Telegram, or Patreon.
- Postiz or a small API client (Breakreach, OmniSocials, PostEverywhere) is the closest off-the-shelf version of “X + Discord + Telegram,” and the hold would be a draft or an approval event the operator still has to wire up.
- Building the announcement text from a changelog is not a feature any of these products advertise. The RSS item’s title and link would be the draft, and a person would edit it.

### Gaps

- Vendor docs were not exhaustively searched for the strings “PyPI” and “changelog” beyond the pricing, approval, RSS, and platform pages fetched. Absence on those pages is strong for the marketing claim, and it is not a full-site search.
- postrss.com is an auto-poster article, not a reviewed social suite with an approval queue. Its destination list in the opening answer does not include X, Telegram, or Patreon.
- No page fetched describes a product that both watches GitHub releases and parks the result for approval.
